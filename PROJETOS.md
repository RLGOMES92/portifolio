## Featured case studies

### 1. VEYRION OS — B2B equipment leasing platform
**Live:** https://veyrion-os.vercel.app/  
**Code:** https://github.com/RLGOMES92/veyrion

A B2B management platform designed to organize the commercial and operational lifecycle of IT equipment leasing.

- Company and contact management
- Sales opportunities and pipeline
- Equipment and inventory workflows
- Proposals, contracts and operational tasks
- Supabase/PostgreSQL data layer and organization-oriented access controls

**Stack:** Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL.

**Business value:** brings commercial and operational information into a centralized workflow, improving visibility and coordination.

**Implementation note:** CRM and messaging integrations listed as planned in the repository are not represented as active production integrations.

### 2. WhatsApp AI Agent — multi-company assistant
**Code:** https://github.com/RLGOMES92/agente

A Node.js project for configurable business assistants using Claude and WhatsApp Cloud API.

- Company-specific configuration
- Conversation history and message queue
- Human handoff and bot pause/resume
- Webhook handling and duplicate-message protection
- Administrative panel and operational health endpoint

**Stack:** Node.js, Claude API, WhatsApp Cloud API, SQLite, Docker.

**Implementation note:** live WhatsApp operation requires valid Meta credentials, webhook configuration and a deployed server. The repository includes local demo/testing paths.
